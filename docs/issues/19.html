China's chip exports doubled in value while the volume rose 3.8 percent.

China's September exports rose 36.3 percent from a year earlier to $447.18 billion, customs data showed on October 14, and the monthly surplus reached $133.53 billion. The computing stack led: integrated-circuit exports were worth $44.95 billion in September, up 136 percent, on 32.65 billion units, up 1 percent. On October 16 Beijing proposed controls on 23 AI technologies, after Taiwan tightened its own chip-export rules. What the readings mean

Volatility measures how much the market is expected to move in the near term compared with the longer term, so a lower reading means less immediate stress. The yield curve is the gap between long-term and short-term government borrowing rates, and a steep curve usually points to expected growth rather than recession. When crypto is described as risk-off, investors are stepping back from the most speculative assets, which often serves as an early note of caution beneath a calm market.
