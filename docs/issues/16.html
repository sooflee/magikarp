Anthropic and OpenAI cut frontier prices on the same day, the same week Xiaomi released the top-ranked open-weight model.

On September 22 Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output, a fifth below Opus 5, and OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol at $2 and $10, half GPT-5.6 Sol's promotional price, alongside a smaller Luna at $0.10 and $0.50. Because lookups are rarely blocked, data can be smuggled out inside the names being looked up. lookups, its second pause in under three months.

On September 23 S&P Global's flash index of US business activity came in at 58.4, its highest since July 2021, and the 10-year Treasury yield rose 15 basis pointsA basis point is one hundredth of a percentage point, so 26 basis points is 0.26 percentage points. to close at 5.11 percent. What the readings mean

Volatility measures how much the market is expected to move in the near term compared with the longer term, so a lower reading means less immediate stress. The yield curve is the gap between long-term and short-term government borrowing rates, and a steep curve usually points to expected growth rather than recession. When crypto is described as risk-off, investors are stepping back from the most speculative assets, which often serves as an early note of caution beneath a calm market.

The wildcard · Fraud, cyber and crime

A week after hijacking a rival gang's leak site, ShinyHunters breaks into the FBI's jobs portal and calls it a defence of its business.

The week before, on the night of September 18, ShinyHuntersAn English-speaking extortion group active since 2020, behind the 2024 thefts from Snowflake customers and a string of 2025 breaches of Salesforce customer data. took over the leak site of Cl0pA Russian-speaking ransomware and extortion gang known for mass campaigns through file-transfer software such as MOVEit in 2023., another extortion gang, through a file-upload flaw in the Grav software that ran it, and demanded an eight-figure sum and an apology, threatening to name the companies that paid Cl0p during its Oracle E-Business Suite campaign. On September 22 it told 404 Media 'We hacked the FBI', and told BleepingComputer it got in through an unknown flaw in Oracle PeopleSoft; the FBI said it was investigating activity affecting FBIjobs.gov. The group told The Register the FBI breach 'was fundamentally a public relations and marketing initiative for our business.' Three days later a Seattle court sentenced a US soldier from the 2024 Snowflake extortion crew to 70 months.

ShinyHunters hacks Cl0p's leak site, threatens to extort the ransomware gang (BleepingComputer, September 19)
The group says it took Cl0p's source code, logs and the private keys to its Tor site, and that the Oracle exploit Cl0p used in 2025 was its own work. No outlet has yet confirmed that the list of Cl0p's payers was published.

We hacked the FBI: hackers say they have data on all FBI employees (404 Media, September 22)
404 Media reviewed a sample covering about 5,000 alleged agents, with names, home addresses, phone numbers and spouses' details. The FBI told BleepingComputer it is 'aware of claims regarding unauthorized activity affecting FBIjobs.gov'; ShinyHunters says the hack answered a May FBI alert about the group.

US soldier gets 70 months in prison for AT&T and Verizon extortions (Krebs on Security, September 25)
Cameron Wagenius, 22, stole call and text records on more than 100 million AT&T customers in 2024 from Snowflake accounts without multi-factor authentication. Prosecutors said he made about $1,500 from selling data.

Critical Citrix NetScaler zero-days exploited (CISA, September 27)
CISA says two of eight newly disclosed flaws, each able to enable remote code execution on its own, are under attack worldwide. It tells operators to check for compromise before patching, because updating may destroy forensic evidence.
